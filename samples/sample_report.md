# Water Reuse Outlook 2026 (SAMPLE, fictional data for demo purposes)

Published by: Example Water Institute (fictional)

## Executive summary

Europe treats most of its municipal wastewater, but reuses very little of it. Across the 14 countries surveyed, only 2.4% of treated municipal wastewater was reused in 2025, while 61% of utilities reported at least one drought-related supply restriction in the past five years.

## Cost and energy

Reclaimed water is cheaper than many decision makers assume. In the surveyed plants, the median cost of producing water fit for irrigation reuse was €0.38 per cubic metre, compared with €0.92 per cubic metre for seawater desalination.

Energy recovery is the most under-used lever. Plants that installed anaerobic digestion with combined heat and power covered on average 47% of their own electricity demand.

## Barriers

Regulation, not technology, is the main brake. 72% of utility managers named permitting and unclear quality rules as the biggest barrier to reuse, ahead of financing (41%) and public acceptance (23%).

## Outlook

If the reuse share reached the level already achieved by the leading country in the survey, an additional 1.1 billion cubic metres of water per year would be available for agriculture and industry.
